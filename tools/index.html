Free Study Tools

Every tool below is built on the official grading rules used by Bangladeshi education boards, the UGC, and the IELTS band descriptors. No signup, no data collection — every calculation runs in your browser.

SSC GPA Calculator

School — Calculate your SSC GPA for Science, Commerce, and Arts groups across all Bangladesh education boards. Uses official grading rules.

HSC GPA Calculator

College — Calculate your HSC GPA for Science, Business Studies, and Humanities. Supports both grade-based and marks-based entry.

University CGPA Calculator

University — Semester-wise GPA tracking and cumulative CGPA calculation for all Bangladesh universities. Includes target GPA planner.

IELTS Band Score Calculator

Test Prep — Calculate your overall IELTS band for Academic and General Training. Convert raw Listening and Reading scores to band scores.
