      How this calculator works

      Bangladesh follows the University Grants Commission (UGC) 4.00 scale: A+ = 4.00, A = 3.75, A− = 3.50, B+ = 3.25, B = 3.00, B− = 2.75, C+ = 2.50, C = 2.25, D = 2.00, F = 0.00. Unlike SSC and HSC, university CGPA is credit-weighted — each course is multiplied by its credits, summed, and divided by total credits attempted. The target GPA planner tells you what semester GPA you need to reach a future CGPA goal, given your current CGPA and completed credits.

      How to use it

      1. Enter each course's credits and the letter grade you received.
      2. Add as many courses or semesters as needed — the calculator tracks cumulative totals.
      3. Use the target-GPA planner to enter your goal CGPA and remaining credits, and the calculator tells you the required semester GPA.

      Frequently asked questions

      Why is my university CGPA out of 4 and not 5?

      Bangladeshi universities follow the UGC standard, which uses a 4.00 scale. SSC and HSC use a separate 5.00 scale set by the Ministry of Education.

      How is CGPA different from semester GPA?

      Semester GPA is for one semester only. CGPA is the credit-weighted average across all semesters you have completed.

      Does the calculator handle retakes?

      Most universities replace the lower grade with the higher one. If your university uses this rule, only enter the retake grade — not both attempts.

      What CGPA is considered good in Bangladesh?

      3.50+ is strong, 3.00–3.49 is solid, below 3.00 limits scholarship and job options. For study abroad, 3.25+ is often the practical floor.

      Can I convert CGPA to percentage with this tool?
